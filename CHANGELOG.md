## 1.0.0

- Initial release of `interactive_radar_chart`.
- **Draggable Vertices**: Interactive handles allowing users to grab and drag polygon corners along radial axes to adjust values in real-time.
- **Magnetic Snapping & Step Intervals**: Snaps to designated step intervals (e.g., 1–5 stars or increments of 10) with subtle haptic ticks.
- **Multi-Layer Comparison**: Displays reference/benchmark silhouettes (read-only) overlaid against the editable user polygon.
- **Polygon & Circular Webs**: Web concentric polygons or concentric circular rings with customizable level divisions.
- **Rich Theming**: Dark cyber slate, clean light, gaming neon, and custom color palettes.
- **Multi-Platform Support**: 100% pure Dart & Flutter CustomPainter with zero platform channels.
